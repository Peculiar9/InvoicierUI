invoicier.

Your invoices, your tax return.

Invoicing built for Nigerian freelancers and small businesses. Send a branded invoice from your phone in about thirty seconds. Your client taps a link and pays — bank transfer, domiciliary dollar account, Wise or USDT — with no app and no signup. Every payment lands tax-grade: income on the day the money arrived, VAT worked out per invoice, withholding tax saved as credit records. When March comes, the books are already done.

  • Invoices carry your own numbering (ADA-0001, not INV-), your logo and colours.
  • Payment details never travel in email — they live only on the secure payment page.
  • Paid in naira, dollars, euros or pounds; official FX rates snapshotted the day money lands.
  • Receipts generate and email themselves the moment a payment is confirmed.
  • Invoicing is free, and stays free. The filing pack is the product, priced beside the ₦100k late-filing penalty.
